About Panditji Ka Dhaba

पंडित जी का ढाबा · Tilak Road, Dehradun

Panditji Ka Dhaba is a pure vegetarian dhaba on Tilak Road, Dehradun, in front of PNB Bank and beside Manbhavan Road. Every item is prepared without onion and garlic.

Our signature dish is Chole Samosa.

  • Phone orders and delivery
  • Daily breakfast, lunch and dinner tiffin
  • Bulk orders for birthdays and parties
